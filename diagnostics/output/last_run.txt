### Retry-path demonstrations

- two 503s then a memento, retries=2: success on attempt 3, archived status 200 (HTTP attempts: 3)
- persistent 503, retries=2: MementoPlaybackError after 3 attempts (HTTP attempts: 3)
- RemoteDisconnected on every attempt, retries=2: ConnectionError: ('Connection aborted.', RemoteDisconnected('Remote end closed connection without response')) (note: not treated as retryable) (HTTP attempts: 1)
